What the suite does, in three lines

01 — Match

Field by field

GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head compared separately. A match means the same invoice, not a similar one.

02 — Explain

Reason on every row

Matched, value differs, missing in 2B, duplicate, wrong-state GSTIN. Filter on the reason and the exceptions are the only thing left.

03 — Export

Two-sheet Excel

A summary of what reconciled and an exceptions sheet carrying the reason into the file — not just the screen.
