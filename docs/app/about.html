A practice that got tired of matching by eye

Fingenuity Associates is an accounts and GST practice in Indore. The suite started as our own month-end tool. It is still the tool we use.

Who we are

Written inside a working practice, not a product studio

We file GST returns, keep books and reconcile stock for a book of clients across Indore and nearby districts. Every month, the same two hours went into comparing one export against another in Excel — sorting, eyeballing invoice numbers, and still missing the duplicate that had been claimed twice.

So we wrote the comparison down properly. GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head get compared field by field. One invoice pairs once. Anything that does not tie gets a reason you can filter on, and the whole thing exports as a summary sheet plus an exceptions sheet.

Then other practices asked for it. That is the only reason this is a website and not a folder on our own laptop.

How we work

Four steps, same on every module

The flow does not change between GST, bank, TDS and stock. Learn it once and every module behaves the same way.

1

Bring both files

The two exports go in as they are — purchase register and 2B, bank statement and cash book, stock register and books. They are read in your browser.

2

Match field-wise

GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head are compared. One invoice pairs once, inside the ₹1 tolerance you set.

3

Review the exceptions

Every row carries a filterable reason — matched, value differs, missing in 2B, duplicate. Filter, and the review is a short list.

4

Export and file

An Excel with a summary sheet and an exceptions sheet. The working paper is done, and the numbers on it can be traced back to a row.

Come see it run on your own file

Bring one client's two exports. We will run the reconciliation on a shared screen and you can judge the exceptions list yourself.
