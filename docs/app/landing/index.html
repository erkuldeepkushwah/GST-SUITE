What we do

Three things, done properly

The suite does not try to be an accounting package. It does the one job that eats your team's evenings — matching two records and explaining what does not agree.

Match, field by field

GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head are compared column by column. A match means the same invoice — not a similar one.

A reason on every row

Matched, value differs, missing in 2B, duplicate — each line carries a reason you can filter on, so the exceptions are the only thing left on screen.

An Excel you can file

One workbook, two sheets: a summary of what reconciled, and an exceptions sheet with the reason on each row. No re-typing into a working paper.

Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about privacy, matching mechanics, and report export formats.

Does our client data leave our computer?01

No, absolutely not. Fingenuity runs 100% on a client-side WebAssembly/JavaScript calculation engine in your browser. Your files are parsed in local memory; no financial rows, GSTINs, or figures are ever uploaded or stored on any remote cloud server.

Can we set custom matching tolerance for pennies and round-offs?02

Yes. You have complete control to set the monetary tolerance from ₹0.00 up to ₹10.00. Most practices use ₹1.00 so fractional differences caused by software rounding algorithms do not end up flagged as false exceptions.

How does it handle multi-rate invoices (e.g. 5%, 12%, 18% in one bill)?03

The suite automatically aggregates tax heads (CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess) at invoice level while preserving line-level item tags. If the total bill matches but individual rates are booked differently in your ledger, the reason code explicitly states “Tax rate distribution variance”.

What format reports are generated?04

You receive a standard, formula-free .xlsx workbook with two worksheets: Summary (totals, category breakdown, count of invoices) and Exceptions (every unmatched or mismatched invoice with an exact filterable reason).
