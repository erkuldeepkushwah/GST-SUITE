About us

A practice that got tired of matching by eye

Fingenuity Associates is an accounts and GST practice in Indore. The suite started as our own month-end tool — and it is still the tool we use.

Written inside a working practice, not a product studio. Every module earns its place by saving a real evening.

We file GST returns, keep books and reconcile stock for a book of clients across Indore and nearby districts. Every month the same two hours went into comparing one export against another in Excel — sorting, eyeballing invoice numbers, and still missing the duplicate that had been claimed twice.

So we wrote the comparison down properly. GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head get compared field by field. One invoice pairs once. Anything that does not tie gets a reason you can filter on, and the whole thing exports as a summary sheet plus an exceptions sheet.

Our team of experts will help you in all situations

The practice runs two desks. One lives in the reconciliations — GST, bank, TDS, stock. The other handles compliance and advisory, and is the desk that ends up answering the notice when something was missed three months ago.

Because both sit in the same room, the tool and the filing are never out of step. If a matching rule turns out to be wrong in practice, it gets fixed that week rather than at the next release.

Our desks

Reconciliation desk

GST · Bank · TDS · Stock

This is where the suite is used hardest. Purchase registers against 2A and 2B, GSTR-1 against 3B, statements against cash books, stock registers against the books. Every month, for every client, with the exceptions written down rather than remembered.

Compliance & advisory desk

Returns · Notices · Working papers

Files the returns, answers the notices, and keeps the working papers that a notice asks for. Because the reconciliation output is already a two-sheet Excel, the working paper is done at the same time as the reco — not reconstructed later under a deadline.

Our values

Field-wise, not fuzzy

GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head compared separately. A match means the same invoice, not a similar one.

Explain every row

Every line says why it is there — matched, value differs, missing in 2B, duplicate. A number without a reason is not a working paper.

Your files stay yours

The reconciliation runs in the browser. Client sheets are read on your machine and are not uploaded to us.
