Privacy note

Your client data is not our business model

The reconciliation itself runs in the browser. When you open a module and pick two files, those files are read on your own machine — they are not uploaded to a server of ours, and they are not stored anywhere after the tab is closed.

  • Files stay in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no copy on our side to lose.
  • This form is the only thing we receive — your name, firm, contact details and what you wrote.
  • No client identifiers in the message. Keep GSTINs, ledger extracts and party names out of the enquiry box.
  • Ask us to delete your enquiry at any time and we will, along with the reply thread.
