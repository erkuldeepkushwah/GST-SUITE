Questions

Everything a practice asks before the first demo

Ten answers about the files, the matching rules, the report and where your data sits. If yours is not here, write to us — we answer practice questions whether or not you use the suite.

01

Does our client data leave our computer?

No. The reconciliation runs in the browser — when you pick the two files, they are read on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded to us, and nothing is kept after the tab is closed. The enquiry form on the contact page is the only thing we ever receive.

02

Which files do we need to start?

Two exports per module. For GST 2A/2B that is the portal download and your purchase register. For GSTR-1 and 3B it is the filed GSTR-1 and the 3B figures. Bank, TDS and stock take the two records you would otherwise compare in Excel. Excel, CSV and JSON all work.

03

Can we set our own matching tolerance?

Yes. The tolerance is configurable from ₹0 upward, and most practices settle on ₹1 so that round-off differences do not show up as exceptions. The tolerance applies to both the taxable value and each tax head.

04

What happens when the invoice matches but the GSTIN does not?

That is reported as a possible mismatch, not a silent match and not a plain mismatch. The reason reads “book entry invoice booked in wrong state GSTIN”, and the row carries both registrations with their state names — for example portal 27… (Maharashtra) against books 24… (Gujarat). It is one invoice, so it appears once.

05

Can one invoice be matched twice?

No. An invoice pairs once and once only, so the same bill cannot be counted twice in the summary. Duplicates in either file are surfaced with their own reason instead of being matched away.

06

What does the Excel report contain?

Two sheets. A summary of what reconciled, module by module, and an exceptions sheet where every row carries its reason — matched, value differs, missing in 2B, duplicate, wrong-state GSTIN. The reason travels into the file, not just the screen.

07

Which modules are included?

Six: GST 2A/2B against the purchase register, GSTR-1 against GSTR-3B, GST-Reconciliation from any portal file, bank statement against cash book, TDS against 26AS and Form 16A, and stock register against the books.

08

Is the interface available in Hindi?

Yes — five UI languages: English, हिन्दी, 中文, Español and Français. The language setting applies across every module, and the exported column headings stay in English so the working paper reads the same for everyone.

09

Do we need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser, so there is no desktop install, no ODBC plugin and no Excel add-in. That matters in a practice where the same file may be opened on a partner's laptop and an article's desktop on the same evening.

10

How do we try it on our own data?

Send us one client's two exports and we will run the reconciliation on a shared screen. No upload, no trial key, no setup call — you watch the exceptions list build and decide afterwards.
