Common to every module

Four things you get whichever module you open

The modules differ in what they read, not in how they behave. That is deliberate — a new joiner should not have to learn six interfaces.

Field-wise matching

GSTIN, invoice number, date, taxable value and each tax head compared separately — not a fuzzy name match.

A filterable reason

Every row says why it is there. Filter to "value differs" and the review is a short, finite list.

One invoice, one pair

An invoice can be matched once and once only, so nothing is counted twice in the summary.

Excel out, every time

A summary sheet and an exceptions sheet, with the reason carried into the file — not just the screen.
